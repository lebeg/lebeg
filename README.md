## About Me

- **Twenty-two years** from first line of code to CTO. C++ games and maps at Nokia and HERE, deep learning at Amazon, ML platforms at Sony, then the business side.
- **Partner & CTO at Hugly** (2024–2026): co-owned the P&L of a 5M-user consumer platform and took recurring revenue from **US$150K to US$530K+ a month** (US$6M+ ARR) in two years, then sold my stake.
- **Amazon's AI expertise centre** (2016–2020): led a team of 10 on deep learning frameworks and ML infrastructure, and cut CI/CD costs from **US$600K to US$150K a month** while scaling to hundreds of daily jobs.
- **Co-founder of [Aenima Labs](https://aenimalabs.com)**, an R&D lab that began as my INSEAD Global Executive MBA'27 final project. We work on agent memory, agent testing and Kodama, a consented way to preserve how a person thinks.
- Since 2020 I run **Chernov Consulting**: AI platform builds and technology turnarounds for Sony, Verisk Maplecroft and Silicon Valley startups, every client through referrals.
